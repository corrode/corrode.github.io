Planning your Rust conference trips for 2027? Here are the events with confirmed dates so far. We'll add more conferences, ticket prices, and call for proposals (CFP) deadlines as organizers announce them.

Come say hi if you see us at any of these events! (We'll bring [Rust in Production](/podcast) stickers.) For the 2026 events, see [Rust Conferences 2026](/blog/rust-conferences-2026/).

## Q1 2027

### Rust Nation UK (London, UK)

[![Rust Nation UK](rustnation.jpg)](https://www.rustnationuk.com/)

Rust Nation returns to London with a one-day, three-track conference at a new venue in the City of London. A separate workshop day is being planned for February 17, but is not yet confirmed.

- **When**: February 18, 2027
- **Where**: 1 Basinghall Avenue, London, UK
- **Format**: 1 day of talks across 3 tracks
- **Focus**: Rust developers of all experience levels
- **Tickets**: Early-bird tickets are on sale; workshop tickets will be announced separately.
- **CFP**: [Announced as open](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/rust-nation_rustlang-rust-activity-7497654419044384768-9TS4); no closing date confirmed yet.
- **Links**: [Website](https://www.rustnationuk.com/) | [Tickets](https://tickets.rustnationuk.com/events/vitisevents/2366632)

## Q2 2027

### TokioConf (Portland, USA)

[![TokioConf](tokioconf.jpg)](https://www.tokioconf.com/)

After its first edition in 2026, TokioConf returns to Portland. The talks focus on practical Rust: building and shipping software, architecture, testing, performance, and working with Tokio.

- **When**: April 26-27, 2027
- **Where**: The Armory, Portland, Oregon, USA
- **Focus**: Practical Rust and Tokio
- **CFP**: [Open](https://sessionize.com/tokioconf-2027/) — closes November 30, 2026 at 11:59 PM Pacific.
- **Links**: [Website](https://www.tokioconf.com/) | [CFP Announcement](https://tokio.rs/blog/2026-10-06-tokioconf-2027-cfp)

### RustWeek (Utrecht, Netherlands)

[![RustWeek](rustweek.jpg)](https://2027.rustweek.org/)

RustWeek brings the Rust community together in Utrecht for talks, workshops, a hackathon, and social activities. The week also includes the invitation-only Rust Project All Hands.

- **When**: May 24-29, 2027
- **Where**: Utrecht, Netherlands (talks at Kinepolis; workshops at Beatrixgebouw)
- **Format**: 1 day of workshops, 2 days of talks, a hackathon, and social activities
- **Focus**: Broad, open to Rust developers of all levels
- **Pricing**: Individual tickets are €199, with an additional €100 discount available for those who can't afford the full price ([organizer announcement](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/rustnl_rustweek-2027-rustweek-2027-activity-7503799563850452992-jA0N)).
- **CFP**: [Open](https://sessionize.com/rustweek-2027) — closes January 10, 2027 at 23:59 CET. Workshop proposals are handled separately via [email](mailto:rustweek@rustnl.org).
- **Links**: [Website](https://2027.rustweek.org/) | [CFP Announcement](https://2027.rustweek.org/blog/2026-10-01-cfp-opened/)

## Q3 2027

### RustConf (Vancouver, Canada + Online)

[![RustConf](rustconf.jpg)](https://rustconf.com/)

Hosted by the Rust Foundation, RustConf heads to Vancouver in 2027, with online participation available too. More details about the program are still to come.

- **When**: September 7-10, 2027
- **Where**: Vancouver, Canada, and online
- **Focus**: Rust and its open-source ecosystem
- **Links**: [Website](https://rustconf.com/)

## Q4 2027

### EuroRust (Prague, Czech Republic + Online)

EuroRust heads to Prague for two days of talks covering Rust patterns and idioms, systems programming, CLI tooling, servers, and WASM. Workshops take place on October 13 at a separate location in the city center, with side activities on October 13, 14, and 16.

- **When**: October 13-16, 2027 (main conference October 14-15)
- **Where**: Cubex Centrum, Prague, Czech Republic, and online
- **Format**: 1 day of workshops, 2 days of talks, and side activities
- **Focus**: The European Rust community, covering all things Rust
- **Tickets**: Early-bird tickets are on sale. Reduced-price tickets are available for private attendees and freelancers; company-paid attendees need company tickets. Side activities are ticketed separately.
- **Links**: [Website](https://eurorust.eu/) | [Tickets](https://www.tickettailor.com/events/eurorust/2414461)
