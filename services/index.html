Here’s the problem…

Your existing system is showing its age and starting to become a liability. You want to move to Rust, but you’re unsure where to start and which parts of the system should be rewritten first. Along the way, your team needs to build the expertise to maintain the new Rust codebase and review changes.

And your existing system has to keep running during the migration.

Or you’ve already built a Rust prototype, but you’re not sure whether the architecture is ready for production.

All of these decisions are made with limited information, under budget and time constraints, and you’re worried about making the wrong call. That’s a big task!

There Must Be a Better Way…

What you need is to move forward with confidence. You want excellent Rust that’s robust, testable, quick to compile, and easy for your team to extend. You want Rust projects that are a joy to work with.

You don’t have to figure it all out alone. With an experienced Rust specialist alongside your team, you can review the architecture and build your team’s expertise as you go.

For an existing C++ or Python system, we find the component that makes the most sense to migrate first and plan how to integrate it with the rest of the system. We work on a deployment strategy that allows you to roll back if something goes wrong. We also establish CI and review workflows that your team can maintain after the migration is complete.

Your team will spend thousands of hours maintaining Rust services. Invest the time to learn how to do it well.

Why Me?

I’m Matthias Endler. I’ve been part of the Rust community since 2015 and have worked as a consultant since 2020. I focus on Rust migrations, architecture reviews, and getting teams up to speed with production-level Rust expertise.

I maintain open-source Rust projects including lychee and host the Rust in Production podcast, where I speak with engineers about the systems they build and maintain. You’ll work with me directly, in your team’s codebase and existing workflows.

I teach Rust from first principles. Together, we model your domain, design APIs, and use design patterns that make refactoring and testing easy.

Let’s Find The Right Support

We start with a free consultation to discuss your current system, your team’s experience, and your goals. From there, we find the right support for your team. We plan the hands-on migration work and mentoring requirements at this stage.

1. Assess

In this stage, we review your system, architecture, and migration options against your operational constraints. We identify the risks, choose a manageable first step, and agree on what to tackle next and how we’ll work together.

2. Migrate

Alongside your engineers, I guide implementation through pairing, architecture discussions, and code reviews. This also covers dependency choices, testing strategies, and project structure. At all times, your team stays involved in the decisions and the code.

3. Enable

The goal is for your engineers to explain, extend, and operate the system without depending on me for every decision. I do this by giving workshops, reviewing code, and offering 1-on-1 mentoring. Your engineers practice designing APIs and writing production-grade Rust while I provide feedback and guidance.

Happy To Answer Your Questions

How do I know you're any good?

You can inspect my open-source work, including lychee, tinysearch, and hyperjson. For a sense of how I approach technical decisions, listen to the Rust in Production podcast or watch one of the talks linked above.

What is your main area of expertise within Rust?

Backend systems, APIs, command-line tooling, and DevOps. I help teams with architecture, idiomatic Rust, testing, and deployment, combining hands-on development with training and code reviews.

What if we're new to Rust?

That’s perfectly fine! I specialize in helping teams adopt Rust. We can start with training sessions and gradually move into more hands-on development as your team’s comfort with Rust grows.

What size teams do you typically work with?

I’ve worked with teams of various sizes, from small startups to large enterprise teams. My approach is adaptable to your team’s size and structure. Whether you’re a solo developer looking for mentorship or a large team transitioning to Rust, I can tailor my services to suit your needs.

That said, I work best with small, focused groups. My ideal group size is 3-10 people, typically towards the lower end of that range. This allows for more personalized attention and effective knowledge transfer. If you have larger teams, we can arrange multiple sessions with smaller groups to ensure everyone gets the most out of our time together.

How long does a typical project last?

Project durations vary based on scope and complexity. Typically, engagements range from a few weeks for focused tasks to several months for more comprehensive projects. We’ll discuss timelines during our initial consultation.

How will we collaborate?

I’m located in the Central European time zone (CET), and I’m available for regular meetings during business hours.

I work remotely with clients worldwide. However, I’m also open to on-site visits if needed, especially for workshops or kick-off meetings.

What's your availability like?

Tell me your approximate project timing in the inquiry form so we can discuss whether our schedules align.

What's your pricing structure?

I offer flexible booking options for half days or full days. During these booked times, I’ll be fully available for questions and consulting. We’ll discuss and agree on the days in advance, and you have the flexibility to add or remove days as needed. This structure allows us to adapt to your project’s evolving needs.

How do you handle payment?

I accept payment via bank transfer or credit card. We’ll discuss payment terms and schedules during our initial consultation.

What's your cancellation policy?

I understand that plans can change. Therefore, you can cancel our services at the end of any month with no further obligation.

How do you handle confidentiality?

I take confidentiality very seriously. I’m happy to sign NDAs and work within your company’s security protocols to ensure all project details remain confidential.

Do you offer ongoing support after project completion?

Yes, I can provide ongoing support and maintenance as needed. However, the goal is to empower your team to be self-sufficient in Rust development. We can discuss post-project support options based on your specific needs.

What happens if you're unavailable due to illness or other reasons?

As a solo consultant, I strive to maintain clear communication with my clients. In case of planned absences, I’ll notify you well in advance. For unexpected issues, I have a network of trusted Rust experts who can provide backup support if absolutely necessary, ensuring your project stays on track. However, this would only be done with your explicit approval.
